## 🌀 What is this place?

Not a blog. Not a wiki. Not a portfolio.

**Cogitatio Elegans** *(lat. "elegant thought")* is a living field notebook — a single evolving repository where a few enormous ideas keep colliding with each other:

| 🧬 | 🤖 | ⏳ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Human Evolution** | **Technological Leverage** | **Historical Mirrors** |
| Where we came from, biologically and cognitively | Where our tools are taking us, and how far one mind can now reach | Where we've already been — because the past keeps rhyming |

Add a dash of 🔬 **scientific discovery** stirred through all three, and you get something closer to a *telescope pointed both backward and forward at once.*

---

## 🗺️ Why "elegans"?

Because good thinking, like good code, should be **economical** — few assumptions, maximum insight. This repo is an exercise in cutting ideas down to their most elegant form, then letting them evolve in the open.

> 🧩 Think of it as a compounding interest account for curiosity. Small deposits of insight, left to accrue.
